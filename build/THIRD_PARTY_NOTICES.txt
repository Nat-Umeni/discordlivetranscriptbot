SPEECH MODEL (not included)
---------------------------

The speech model isn't part of this download. The first time the bot runs,
it downloads NVIDIA's Nemotron Speech Streaming 0.6B (560 ms, int8), as
converted to ONNX by the sherpa-onnx project, straight from its publisher:
  https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj2/sherpa-onnx-nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b-560ms-int8-2026-04-25
The model is NVIDIA's, under the NVIDIA Open Model License:
  https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/agreements/enterprise-software/nvidia-open-model-license/
  Original model: https://huggingface.co/nvidia/nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b
